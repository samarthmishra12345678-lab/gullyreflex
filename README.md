# 🏏 GullyReflex
### *"Gully se Ground Tak"* — your pocket sports coach

A desi reflex & agility trainer that turns **any phone into a coach** — built in the PeddieHacks 2026 hackathon window by a 17-year-old from Ahilyanagar, India, for every kid who has the passion but no coach, no ground, and no equipment.

**One self-contained HTML file. Fully offline. No installs, no accounts, no internet.**

---

## 🎮 The Drills

| Drill | What it trains |
|---|---|
| ⚡ **Green Tap** | Raw reaction time (ms) — the same light-drill used in F1 & cricket training, with a **YOU vs THE WORLD** benchmark (You vs Avg Adult 273ms vs Pro Athlete 200ms) |
| 🎯 **Target Smash** | 20-second hand-eye coordination burst |
| 🧤 **Keeper Catch** | Wicket-keeper drill — real ball physics: gravity, curves, and a glove that follows your finger |
| 🧭 **Agility Arrows** | Direction-change speed — swipe the arrow before the timer dies |
| 🏃 **Sprint Shake** | Motion-sensor drill — the phone's accelerometer counts your reps as you shake/sprint in place. No camera, no wearable, no internet |
| 🌬️ **Recovery Breath** | Guided box breathing (4-4-4-4) — the recovery ritual pro athletes use between overs. Calm mind = fast reflexes |

## 🫡 The Coach System

- **Daily Missions** — the app sets your training routine every day; complete it for bonus XP
- 🔥 **Streaks** — skip a day, lose the flame
- ⭐ **XP & Levels** — every drill levels you up
- 📈 **Reaction History Chart** — watch your reflexes literally get faster
- ⚔️ **Challenge Card** — one tap copies a WhatsApp challenge to flex your score (viral loop, no internet needed)
- ⚡ **5-MIN RUSH** — a chained micro-workout circuit (Smash → Sprint → Tap → Arrows → Breath) for busy mornings
- 🤺 **Gully Battle 2P** — two players, one phone: pass-and-play reaction duels, best of 3
- 💧 **Hydration Meter** — 8-glasses daily tracker, because recovery is training too
- 🧠 **Sports-science facts** woven into every drill

## 🛠️ Tech

- Vanilla **HTML/CSS/JS** — zero dependencies, zero build tools, zero backend
- **DeviceMotion API** — rep counting with the phone's own accelerometer
- **Canvas** confetti engine, **WebAudio** synthesized sound effects (zero audio files)
- **Pointer Events** — one input layer for mouse + touch on any device
- **localStorage** persistence — all data stays on your phone

## ▶️ Run It

1. Download `index.html`
2. Open it in any browser — that's it. (Works from WhatsApp file-share, Bluetooth, a school PC pen-drive... anywhere.)

Or host it in 30 seconds: drag the file to [Netlify Drop](https://app.netlify.com/drop) → share the link.
